About this book

There's money with your name on it sitting in government databases, settlement funds, and benefit programs right now - waiting for you to claim it.

The Free Money Handbook is the honest, no-hype guide to finding it: how to search unclaimed property across all 50 states through official .gov sites and MissingMoney.com, how to file class-action settlement claims (including the no-proof ones that take three minutes), and how to apply for the benefits that put real dollars back every month - SNAP food assistance, free phone service through Lifeline, Medicaid, and housing assistance waitlists.

Written in plain language from lived experience, this book gives realistic payout expectations, a scam-spotting guide so you never pay a fee or fall for a "government grant" lie, and a step-by-step Weekend Claim Sweep: a two-day plan that runs through everything in order.

No secrets, no guru nonsense. Just the money you're already owed - and how to get it.
