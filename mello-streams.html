About Mello

Mello Streams writes and sings her own songs, all originals, no covers. Her writing is story driven and personal: kitchen work, faith, everyday characters, and the funny side of all of it, in titles like Drama Llamas and Stacked Like a Big Mac. The sound runs wide, from radio ready pop and R&B singles to trap to long form instrumentals, and she releases at a pace most artists never touch: 20+ singles since June 2026, on top of album length releases that include RESURRECTION (2023) and /Dote (2025).

She is self released and keeps full ownership of her work, and she performs live in Los Angeles. Our job is the machine around the music: this showcase, her promotion, and her booking outreach, so the songs reach the audience they deserve.
