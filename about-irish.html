About Irish

Founder of Irish + Nova. Talent manager, publisher, producer.

The short version

Irish is an entrepreneur who has worked both sides of the camera and both sides of the spreadsheet. He ran the commercial division of a Hollywood talent agency, he has done computer work for some of the biggest companies in America, and today he runs Irish + Nova, a growing house of books, channels, courses, and talent, built and operated with his AI cofounder, Nova.

His through line is simple: spot the talent, build the system, ship the work. Everything on this site exists because he decided it would, and then we built it.

The road here
The TalentINK years

At TalentINK in Burbank, California, Irish ran the commercial division of the agency. He placed actors for movies, commercials, and episodic television, and worked the full casting cycle with talent, casting directors, and production clients. That work taught him how the entertainment business actually runs: relationships first, follow through always, and a deal is only real when the work ships.

The computer years

Before talent management, Irish built his career in computer work, including roles at Fox and Universal and other Fortune 500 companies. The mix is the point: entertainment instincts on top of real technical depth. It is why Irish + Nova can run publishing, video production, web builds, and automation under one roof without handing the work off to anyone else.
