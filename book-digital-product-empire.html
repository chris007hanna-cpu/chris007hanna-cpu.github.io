About this book

Make it once. Sell it a thousand times.

Digital Product Empire is the plain-language playbook for building real digital products - spreadsheets, printables, templates, graphics packs, and short ebooks - and selling them on Gumroad and Etsy starting with zero dollars. No design degree, no audience, no startup money required.

Inside: the math that makes digital products the best broke-beginner business, ten ranked product ideas with validation tactics, the complete free toolkit (Google Sheets, Canva, Google Docs), step-by-step build guides for spreadsheets people pay $7 for, printables and graphics packs, and short ebooks that price at $9-$15. Then the business half: setting up Gumroad and Etsy shops, writing product pages that convert, pricing without guessing, getting your first ten sales with zero marketing budget, and growing through bundles, email lists, and affiliates.

Closes with a day-by-day 30-day launch plan that takes you from idea to live product in a month - plus an appendix of 20 ready product ideas with exact build specs and prices.

No hype, no $997 upsells, no fake income screenshots. Just the honest system: build once, sell forever.
