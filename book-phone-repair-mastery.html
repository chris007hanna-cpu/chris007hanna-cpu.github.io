About this book

Your phone broke. The repair shop wants $150. Apple wants $279. This book shows you how to fix it yourself for the cost of the part - and how to turn that skill into real income.

Phone Repair Mastery is the beginner's guide to the repairs that actually matter: cracked screens (iPhone and Samsung), dead batteries, broken charging ports, dead speakers and microphones, stuck buttons, cracked camera lenses, and water damage triage. No experience needed. No soldering. Just a $30 toolkit, clear step-by-step guidance, and the judgment to know which repairs are worth doing.

Every repair includes honest numbers - what the part costs, what to charge, how long it takes - plus the safety rules that keep you (and the phone) in one piece. Then Part 5 shows you how to get paying customers: pricing menus, Facebook Marketplace ads, the intake script, and the "no fix, no fee" policy that wins trust.

Written by Christopher J. Hanna, author of The Phone Flip Playbook. The two books work together: flipping finds the deals, repair unlocks the margin. A $40 cracked phone plus a $25 screen becomes a $120 sale.

Stop paying for repairs. Start getting paid for them.
