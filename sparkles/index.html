About

Sparkles, Jamie Petzel, sings karaoke in Hot Springs, Arkansas, and she is a regular at Boogie's. She is a character singer with real stage presence: her signature number is Nancy Sinatra's "These Boots Are Made for Walkin'", performed live on stage, and she works the whole room when she sings. She is a founding singer of the Singers Division at Irish + Nova Talent Management.
