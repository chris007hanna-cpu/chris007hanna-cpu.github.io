About this book

Electric cars are about to change more in the next two years than they have in the last ten - and the reason is the battery.

Solid-state batteries replace the flammable liquid inside today's EV batteries with a solid material. The result: 1,000+ kilometer (620+ mile) range, virtually zero fire risk, far faster charging, and winter performance that leaves today's EVs in the dust. Chinese automakers are already targeting 2026 for the first all-solid-state cars, Europe gets its first mainstream semi-solid-state EV before the end of the year, and cheap sodium-ion city cars are entering mass production right now.

But should YOU buy an EV today - or wait?

The Solid-State EV Buyer's Guide answers that question in plain, hype-free English. No engineering degree required. You'll learn:

How today's EV batteries actually work, and the one flaw solid-state fixes What 1,000-km range, near-zero fire risk, and 74% cold-weather charge retention mean for real drivers The verified 2026-2027 rollout scoreboard: who's shipping, who's testing, and who's just talking (Chery, Changan, Dongfeng, MG, Mercedes, BMW, Stellantis, Honda, Nissan) Semi-solid vs. all-solid-state: what buyers actually get in 2026 The sodium-ion story: why the cheapest EVs of the future won't use lithium at all A clear buy-now-or-wait decision framework based on your driving, your climate, and your budget What the battery transition does to used EV prices - and how to profit from it Total cost of ownership math: sticker price is the wrong number A 2026-2027 model watchlist and a plain-English glossary

Information current as of October 2026. Production timelines slip - this guide shows you how to read battery news like a pro so you never get fooled by a press release again.

If you're thinking about an electric car in the next few years, read this first.
