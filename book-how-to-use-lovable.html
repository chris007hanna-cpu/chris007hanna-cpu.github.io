About this book

You have an idea for an app. What you do not have is a programmer, a budget for one, or any wish to learn code. Lovable builds real web apps from plain English instructions, and this book teaches you to run it like an owner: what to ask for, how to check what you got, and how to prove every part works with your own eyes.

How to Use Lovable takes a complete beginner from an empty account to one real finished project, entirely on the free plan. The project is a booking page for a local service business, built start to finish: the first prompt written out word for word, the booking form wired to a real database so submissions actually save, the publish step, and the proof, a test booking you find sitting in the database yourself.

Inside the twelve chapters: what Lovable is, what it costs, and what it is honestly not for; setting up your account right on the free plan; a tour of only the interface controls your hands will actually touch; the anatomy of a first prompt that works; the conversation craft of edits, fixes, and getting unstuck without burning credits; pages, sections, and style that make the app look like your business; databases, users, storage, secrets, and analytics for daily use; and the mistakes chapter, the ten standard failures every Lovable builder meets, each with its symptom, its cause, and its fix in order, cheapest first.

Every prompt in the book is printed in full, ready to type. Every chapter ends with exercises that finish in something you can point at. The book makes one promise and keeps it: skill. It makes no promise about money, and it tells you plainly where the tool's ceiling is and what to do when you reach it.

This is Book One of the AI Black Belt Field Guides from Hanna Press, the series that teaches one AI tool per book, each with one original animal on the cover. Lovable's animal is the beaver, the builder. The free companion tutorial on the AI Black Belt channel builds this book's project on screen, and the Irish and Nova Academy module walks the same ground as guided labs for readers who want a coach in the room.

If you run a small business, a trade, a studio, or a one person service and you keep saying you need a website that actually does something, this is your first build. Open it with Lovable in front of you and finish with a working app at a live address.
