About this book

Broke is not a business plan - but it's a starting point.

The $0 Startup shows you how to launch a real business with literally zero dollars: no savings, no credit, no investors, no excuses. Just your phone, your time, and one paying customer.

Inside: five business models that genuinely cost $0 to start (service flipping, phone flipping and repair, phone freelancing, faceless content channels, food), the exact playbook for landing your first 3 customers free, how to get paid and track every dollar on paper, when to finally spend your first $100 (and where), and the 7 traps that kill $0 startups - so you walk around them.

No hype, no "six figures in six weeks." Just realistic numbers, copy-paste scripts, and a 7-day launch challenge that ends with your first dollar in one week.

If you've ever said "I need money to make money," this book is your answer.
