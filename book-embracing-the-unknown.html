About this book

A job loss. A deferred dream. A life that stops following the plan. When certainty falls apart, most people freeze - this book teaches you to move.

Embracing the Unknown is a guide to facing life's uncertainties with grit, faith, and the American spirit. Across ten chapters, Christopher J. Hanna dismantles the illusion of control and replaces it with two tools that actually work: curiosity, which turns the unknown into a lantern instead of a threat, and resilience, the backbone that lets you bend without breaking. You'll learn to reframe failure as tuition, make value-based decisions when the path is unclear, strengthen relationships through uncertainty, and adapt to change - new jobs, new homes, new seasons of life - without losing yourself.

Written for hardworking people who believe in self-reliance and personal responsibility, this is practical wisdom drawn from real experience - no platitudes, no victimhood, just a clear-eyed path through the fog. Step into the unknown with curiosity as your guide and resilience as your shield.
