About

Mello Streams writes and sings her own songs, all originals. Her songs tell real stories: kitchen work, faith, everyday characters, with humor running through titles like Drama Llamas and Stacked Like a Big Mac. The range is wide, from radio ready pop and R&B to trap to long form instrumentals, and the pace is relentless: 20+ singles since June 2026 alone. She is self released, owns her work outright, and performs live in Los Angeles.
