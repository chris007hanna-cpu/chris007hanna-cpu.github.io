FOR THOSE WHO SERVED

Their photo in the film. No names, just honored.

A FILM BUILT FROM YOUR PHOTOS
We are making a Veterans Day sleep film for our channel, What We Do In Darkness. It is called Rain on Tent Canvas, For Those Who Served: one calm night of rain on canvas, built to honor the men and women who served. We want it filled with real faces, including yours. Send us a photo of someone who served, and we will weave it into the film.
What to send
THE PHOTO
One clear photo of the person who served, or of their grave or headstone. In uniform, on duty, or a favorite portrait. Any era, any branch. Veterans and the fallen are both welcome here.
WORDS AND MORE
A favorite quote, a letter, a poem, or their story in your words: anything you want honored alongside them is welcome. Send words on their own through our Send It To Nova page and pick Quotes, letters, and words.
NO NAMES, JUST HONORED
Photos appear in the film with no names attached and nothing announced. They are simply there, honored, the way a photograph on the mantel is.
THE DATES
Please send photos by Sunday, November 1, 2026, so we can finish the film in time. It premieres on Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2026, on What We Do In Darkness.
