About this book

Warning: this book will make you groan. Then laugh. Then groan again - louder.

Dad Jokes: 300 So-Bad-They're-Good packs three hundred brand-new, family-friendly dad jokes into twelve laugh-loaded chapters: Animals, Food, Science, School, Sports, Holidays, Music, Ocean & Sea, Space, Work & Jobs, Technology, and Nature & Outdoors. Every joke is 100% original - no recycled internet one-liners, no stale classics, just fresh puns and silly Q&A gags written to earn maximum eye-rolls.

It also includes "How to Tell a Dad Joke (Without Getting Booed)," a hilarious crash course in comedic delivery, so you can deploy each joke with total confidence. Perfect for dads, granddads, uncles, teachers, and anyone who has ever been told "please stop" mid-punchline.

Makes the ideal gift for Father's Day, birthdays, stocking stuffers, and white-elephant exchanges. Buy one for the dad in your life - and one for yourself, because you know you're going to steal his material.
