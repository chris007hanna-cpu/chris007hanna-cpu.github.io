About this book

Turn cheap prepaid phones into real side income - starting with $0 to $40 and just an Android phone.

Millions of prepaid phones get sold, cleared out, and abandoned every year - and most people walk right past them. The Phone Flip Playbook shows you how to buy those underpriced phones, clean them up, and resell them for $20 to $60 profit each, plus how to earn $25 to $40 per job offering legitimate carrier unlocking and account-recovery services in your own town.

Written in plain, no-hype language for broke beginners, this guide covers: where to find deals (Marketplace, pawn shops, Walmart clearance, friends' drawers), the real math including eBay fees and shipping, how to check IMEIs and avoid stolen phones, legitimate FRP and SIM-unlock methods with a clear ethics framework, photographing and listing for top dollar, and how to scale without getting scammed. No "get rich quick" promises - just an honest, legal side hustle you can start this week. Includes a one-page quick-start checklist.
