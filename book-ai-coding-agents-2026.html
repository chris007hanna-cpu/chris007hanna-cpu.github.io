About this book

You don't need to be a programmer to build software anymore - you need to know how to direct the AI that writes it for you. AI Coding Agents 2026 is a plain-English guide for complete beginners to the coding agents that went mainstream in the fall of 2026: Meta's Muse Code, Anthropic's Claude Code, and OpenAI's Codex. You'll learn what coding agents actually are, how to set up Muse Code from zero, how to build your first agent-made project in an evening, and how Muse Spark 1.3's pricing tiers really work - including the honest math on standard vs. contributor pricing. Then comes the practical part: five realistic income plays for non-programmers, from digital products and freelance gigs to automation services for local businesses, plus the prompting and safety habits that separate people who ship from people who stall. No hype, no fake earnings claims, no assumed expertise - just a clear walkthrough of the tools reshaping who gets to build software, and how ordinary people can earn with them. Information current as of October 2026.
