The Feng Shui Thinking

Feng shui, in plain terms, is about flow. Energy, people, and paperwork should all move through a room without getting stuck. Here is how this little room does it.

The Command Seat

Her desk already sits in the command position, facing the door area, which is the strongest seat in any office. She sees everyone who comes in, and nobody ever startles her from behind.

A Clear Path In

The two guest chairs that crowded the front of her desk are gone, and the walkway from the door to the desk is completely clear. A clear path is the heart of good feng shui, and clients feel it the second they step in.

Only Today on the Desk

Two paper trays only, one for today and one for this week. Everything older lives in the cabinet or the closet. A desk that holds only today work makes a mind that holds only today work.

Wood, Water, and Warm Light

A living pothos brings the wood element and fresh air energy. A tiny fountain adds water, the symbol of flow and provision, plus a gentle sound that makes hard conversations softer. A warm lamp softens the overhead glare in the work corner of the desk.

One Quiet Shelf

Books stand straight on most of the bookshelf, and one shelf holds just a small plant, a flameless candle, and one framed photo. Resting space for the eyes is a real feng shui tool, and this room earns it.

The Family Wall, Gallery Style

The photos on her wall are the soul of this room. Lining their edges into a loose grid with matching frames turns a sweet scatter into a gallery, and it costs nothing but ten minutes and a little care.
