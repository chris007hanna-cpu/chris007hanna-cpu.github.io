About Mark

Mark Malone makes EDM, the kind of tracks that are built to move a room. He came to us with an idea we liked right away: real life video for his music. Dancers moving to his tracks, cut into visualizer videos that give the songs a face and help them spread. That idea is now the heart of our collaboration.

Here is how it runs. Mark's tracks score videos across our channels, and every use carries his name in the credits and his links in the description, so viewers who hear something they love can find him in one tap. In return, our team builds the real life dancer visualizer videos for his tracks and cross promotes him across our pages and channels. His music gets our audience, our videos get his sound, and both sides grow together.
