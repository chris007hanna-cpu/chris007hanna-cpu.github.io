About Sparkles

Sparkles, Jamie Petzel to her friends, sings karaoke in Hot Springs, Arkansas, and she is a regular at Boogie's, where the room knows her name. She is a character singer: she picks songs with a story and a wink, steps into them, and plays them to the crowd. Her signature number is Nancy Sinatra's "These Boots Are Made for Walkin'", performed live on a bar stage in Little Rock, where she works the stage, moves, dances, and sings it like she means it.

She is a founding singer of our Singers Division, and our job is the machine around the music: this showcase, her promotion, and her booking outreach, so her nights on stage reach the audience they deserve.
