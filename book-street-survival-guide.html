About this book

Nobody plans to end up on the street. It happens fast - a lost job, a breakup, an eviction, a bad month that turns into a worse one. This book is not inspiration. It's instruction.

Surviving on the Street is a practical, no-sugar-coating guide to staying alive and getting out: where to sleep tonight, how to eat free, how to stay clean with no bathroom, how to keep your phone alive, how to stay safe after dark, how to get ID and benefits, how to survive winter, and the step-by-step ladder back to housing.

Inside you'll find:

What to do in the first 24 hours, in order Shelters, safe sleep spots, and what to avoid Free food: soup kitchens, pantries, and SNAP Hygiene without a bathroom, and the $10 kit that covers it Free phone service through the Lifeline program Day labor, gig work, and benefits that pay Replacing lost ID and getting on housing waitlists now Winter survival: layering, warming centers, frostbite and hypothermia signs A national resource directory: 211, 988, food banks, free clinics, veteran help

Written straight, with respect for the reader. No judgment, no lectures - just what works.
