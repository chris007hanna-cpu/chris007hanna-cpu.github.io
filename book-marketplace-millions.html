About this book

Selling on Facebook Marketplace is the simplest side income a beginner can start - no listing fees, no shipping headaches, and cash in hand the same day. Marketplace Millions is the complete plain-language guide to doing it right: what actually sells (furniture, tools, baby gear, bikes, electronics, and more) with realistic price ranges, where to find inventory free or cheap, phone photography that gets clicks, titles and pricing that move items in days, and word-for-word messaging scripts for buyers, hagglers, and no-shows. You'll also learn the ten most common Marketplace scams - overpayment tricks, fake payment screenshots, phishing links, and more - and exactly how to dodge each one. The book closes with shipping and cross-posting basics, a dead-simple notebook bookkeeping system, and a 7-day challenge that takes you from zero to your first live listing. No hype, no get-rich promises - just a repeatable system for turning unused stuff into steady side income, starting today.
