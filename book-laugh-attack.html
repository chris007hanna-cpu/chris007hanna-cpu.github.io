About this book

Need a laugh? You've come to the right place. Laugh Attack packs 500 brand-new, original one-liners into fifteen hilarious chapters covering everything life throws at you: being broke, loving your phone a little too much, surviving Mondays, worshipping coffee, dodging the gym, and laughing at your own family before they laugh at you.

Every single joke was written fresh for this book - no recycled memes, no dusty hand-me-downs, just original comedy you haven't heard before. Perfect for bathroom reading, coffee breaks, boring meetings, long flights, and anyone who collects funny quotes like treasure.

Read a page a day or devour it in one sitting; either way, you'll walk away with an arsenal of one-liners to steal for parties, captions, speeches, and group chats. Warning: side effects include snorting in public, quoting this book constantly, and becoming the funniest person in the room. Pick up Laugh Attack today and laugh like you mean it.
