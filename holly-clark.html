HOLLY CLARK

Photographer and creative, Huntington Beach, California. Life is better by the beach.

About

Holly Clark is a Surf City local with a photographer eye for the coast: golden hour beaches, pier nights, desert weekends, and the people who make Orange County home. She grew up in Garden Grove, went to Rancho Alamitos, and built a life in Huntington Beach. She is the creator of Holly's Guide to Surf City, her own designed guide to the best coffee, brunch, dinner, and day and night spots in town. Her work is warm, real, and unmistakably Southern California.
